## Investing guardrails (from the 2026-09-24 review of a Gemini chat)

Repeat these to Mike when a deal or market decision comes up.

1. **Refi cash is loan-to-value times appraisal, not appraisal minus price.** At 75% LTV, a $160k appraisal gives a $120k loan. That returns about -$5k on the $125k deal and +$5k on the $115k deal, before closing costs. Paper equity is not cash.
2. **Check seasoning before buying for a refi.** Many lenders cap a cash-out refi at the purchase price for the first 6-12 months of ownership. Get the lender's rule in writing before closing.
3. **Only buy with forced equity.** No more turnkey near the appraisal ceiling. Target 60-65% of value all-in, where the value comes from rehab, operations, or terms.
4. **Commercial value is NOI divided by market cap rate.** "60-65% of ARV" for a park or apartment building means 60-65% of stabilized NOI / cap rate. You cannot skip the cap rate.
5. **Ohio wholesaling needs the seller disclosure.** Since 2026-03-02 (SB 155), wholesalers on 1-4 unit homes must give a separate written disclosure in bold 12-point type. Without it the seller can cancel. Build it into every automated contract.
6. **Luxury "wholesaling" is brokerage.** A 2-3% fee on a $10M+ home is an agent's commission. Confirm Florida licensing and referral-fee rules with a real estate attorney before marketing any contract. Expect a large earnest-money deposit at risk.
7. **Pick markets with numbers, not narratives.** For any market, compare price, rent, taxes, insurance, and refi LTV on the same sheet. Distance is an operations problem a licensed property manager can solve. It is not by itself proof of a wrong market.
8. **One cash strategy at a time.** Run one for 30 days, pause the rest.
9. **Remote rehab controls.** Pay the handyman only on photo/video-verified milestones. Keep a backup contractor. Do not rely on a realtor to judge trade work.
10. **Batmasian is not a template for starting at zero.** He had 13 years of Cambridge apartment and condo-conversion experience and capital before Boca in 1983.
